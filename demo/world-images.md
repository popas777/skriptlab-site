## panthers

Tiedosto: `assets/world-panthers.webp`

Viite: `assets/garden.webp`.

Prompt:

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: a standalone landscape image for an immersive illustrated garden hotspot on the SkriptLab website, 1536x1024 landscape 3:2. Reference image 1 is the existing garden and establishes the SAME world, botanical motifs, geography, warm late-afternoon light, color palette and refined cinematic photorealistic literary fantasy style. Generate a NEW camera viewpoint within this world, not a crop, collage, split panel or UI mockup. Real textures, plausible perspective, natural depth. No lettering, numbers, logos, borders, people or interface. Preserve the welcoming, tranquil, slightly enchanted atmosphere. New viewpoint: now standing very close to the same two friendly spotted panthers playing beside a worn brown leather ball on the broad warm stone/gravel garden path. Camera at the animals' eye height, intimate but comfortably wide, one resting in the left foreground and the other gently resting a paw near the ball at center-right. Both clearly visible, anatomically convincing, relaxed ears and soft expressions; rosette-spotted golden fur, NOT striped tigers, no menace. Behind them the same pale marble flowerbed borders, blue delphiniums, blush and white roses, and massive old branching trees. A little dappled golden sunlight in their fur. Show the garden as if we walked several steps forward from the reference. Do not reproduce the original wide composition.
